# Estimated Person Hours
The team used the **Planning Poker estimation methodology** discussed in the course lecture notes to assist in estimating the effort required 
for the Minesweeper project. Planning Poker is a consensus-based estimation technique in which team members independently evaluate the effort 
of a task, reveal their estimates, discuss differences, and re-estimate when necessary until the team reaches a consensus.

For this project, the major requirements were divided into development tasks. The team compared the relative effort of each task based on the 
amount of work, implementation complexity, risk and uncertainty, and expected testing requirements. These comparisons were then used to reach 
a consensus on the estimated person-hours required for each task.
